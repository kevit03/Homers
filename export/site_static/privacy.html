HOMERs privacy

Effective October 28, 2026

HOMERs is a free, personal project for exploring NBA play-by-play. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect or store personal information about you.

What stays on your device

If you switch between light and dark themes, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so it sticks next time. It never leaves your device. There are no cookies.

Services your browser contacts

Like any website, loading HOMERs means your browser sends requests (which include your IP address and browser type) to:

  • Vercel, which hosts the site and keeps standard, short-lived request logs.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for typefaces.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) for a charting library.
  • NBA.com (cdn.nba.com) and Basketball-Reference for player headshots and team logos.
  • ESPN (site.api.espn.com) for live scores on the Live tab.

Each of these handles those requests under its own privacy policy. HOMERs doesn't receive or keep that data.

Not affiliated

HOMERs is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NBA, its teams, ESPN or Basketball-Reference. Team and player names belong to their owners. Win probabilities and predictions are a model's guesses for fun and learning, not betting advice.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes (for example, if analytics are added), this page will be updated first, with a new date above. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
