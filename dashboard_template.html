The name

Why “HOMERs”?

Homer's epics were performed out loud, one line after another, and the audience never knew exactly what came next. That's the job this model has: it reads a game one play at a time (tip-off, miss, rebound, three) and at every moment guesses what happens next and who's going to win.

And in sports talk, a homer is the fan who always roots for the home team. This model's main output is the home team's chance to win. Unlike most homers, it's graded on how honest its odds are: see the Model tab.
